# Frequently asked questions

Sourced from [page 2 of the current Canva site](https://happyhomescleanings.my.canva.site/happy-home-services/page-2). Wording is lightly cleaned for readability; **rewrite** for the new site voice.

---

### Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Many clients provide a code or key. Tell us your preference and we’ll make it easy.

### Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes. We bring the tools and supplies needed. If you prefer something specific, let us know and have it ready.

### Will you watch my kids if I need to step out?

We’re happy to work around your family’s needs, but we **cannot supervise children**. Our focus is on cleaning and providing a safe, efficient service.

### What’s your cancellation policy?

We ask for **48 hours’ notice** if you need to reschedule.

### Do you do laundry or dishes?

Laundry and dishes are **add-on services**. Let us know in advance if you’d like them included.

### What if I forget my appointment or leave my home locked?

If we can’t access your home at the scheduled time and haven’t heard from you, a **lockout or no-show fee** may apply. We’ll try to reach you before leaving.

### Can I tip the cleaners?

Tips are never expected but always appreciated — cash or added to your online payment.

### How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better, especially for move-in/move-out cleanings. We recommend **at least one week in advance** to secure your preferred time.

### Do you offer same-day or emergency cleanings?

Occasionally, depending on team availability. Message us directly for urgent requests.

### Will you clean around clutter or laundry piles?

We may work around large clutter or laundry to avoid moving personal items, sometimes only lightly dusting those areas. For the best results, tidy surfaces beforehand.

### Will my information be kept private?

Yes. Personal information, access codes, and your address are kept secure and confidential.

### Do I need to provide parking?

If you live where parking is limited or permit-only, tell us in advance. Parking fees (if any) may be added to your invoice.

### What types of services do you offer?

Residential, Airbnb, commercial, office, church, move-in/move-out cleanings, pet care, landscape services, and house sitting. *(See [services/](./services/) and [other-services.md](./services/other-services.md) for pricing we have on file.)*

### How do I get a quote?

Email **Haileycartierworks@gmail.com** or call/text **(605) 519-0198** *(confirm phone)*. We’ll ask a few quick questions and provide a free quote or schedule a walkthrough.

### How often can you clean my property?

One-time, weekly, biweekly, monthly, or a **custom schedule**.

### What if I’m not satisfied with the cleaning?

Your satisfaction matters. If something was missed, we’ll come back and make it right at no extra cost, or offer a discount on your next cleaning. *(Also see [policies.md](./policies.md#satisfaction-guarantee) — 24-hour contact window.)*
