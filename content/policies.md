# Policies

## Pets

- Happy to clean homes **with pets**.
- Pets that may be **aggressive or anxious** around strangers must be **secured** (separate room, kennel, or safe area) during the cleaning.
- Clients must **inform us of any pets** before the appointment.
- If a pet creates **unsafe working conditions**, we reserve the right to **stop or reschedule** the cleaning.
- **Pet-friendly cleaning products** available when requested; clients should disclose pet sensitivities or allergies before service.

## Human waste cleanup

For the health and safety of our team, any human feces found outside of the toilet will require an additional **$150 biohazard cleaning fee** if the client would like it cleaned.

If the client chooses not to pay the additional fee, our team will leave the affected area untouched and continue cleaning all other agreed upon areas.
