# Client prep guide

How clients can prepare before a cleaning appointment (from current site — rewrite for tone on new site).

## Before we arrive

1. **Tidy personal items** — Pick up clothes, toys, and belongings so cleaners can focus on cleaning surfaces and floors.
2. **Secure pets** — See [policies.md](./policies.md#pets). Friendly pets may be OK per old site; use the stricter policy from supplementary notes unless Hailey prefers otherwise.
3. **Provide access** — Key, code, or someone **18+** home to let the team in.
4. **Special instructions** — Note fragile items, problem areas, or priorities in advance.
5. **Supplies (optional)** — If you want us to use your products or tools, leave them in an obvious spot. Default: we bring supplies (see FAQ).
6. **Supervise children** — Keep young children in a safe area during the visit.

## Clutter

- Large clutter or laundry piles may limit how thoroughly we can clean; we may work around them or lightly dust. Tidying beforehand improves results (see FAQ).
