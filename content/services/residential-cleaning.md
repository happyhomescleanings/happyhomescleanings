# Residential house cleaning

## Scheduling flexibility

Packages are not limited to weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Options mentioned on the current site include:

- Daily
- Every other day
- Weekly / biweekly / monthly
- Custom schedules

Service address and schedule can be updated as needs change.
